## Hero

1. **NVIDIA BRINGS HUANG BACK** — Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated
   co-founder Jensen Huang as president and CEO, effective immediately,
   11 days after he resigned (12 Nov) over reports of extramarital
   affairs, one involving a woman US authorities suspect of links to
   Chinese intelligence. The board said it was satisfied with his candour
   and, after input from senior US officials, is confident his return
   poses no national security risk. Shares surged ~33%; Nvidia regained
   close to $1 trillion in value; tech stocks rallied.
   Sources: TechCrunch 23 Nov 2026; Morningstar/Dow Jones 23 Nov 2026;
   Bloomberg Law 23 Nov 2026; Nvidia press release via 9to5Mac.

## Grid stories

2. **24 DEAD IN INDONESIA FACTORY FIRE** — A fire tore through the
   PT Fonusa Agung Mulia confectionery factory (Milkita maker) in
   Cikande, Banten, on Saturday evening 21 Nov. Authorities confirmed
   24 dead on Monday 23 Nov; rescuers kept searching the unstable
   wreckage for about 30 people still missing. Preliminary reports point
   to an air-conditioner short circuit; cause under investigation.
   Source: Reuters, 23 Nov 2026 (via BusinessWorld / WT BX).

3. **TRUMP'S HUSH-MONEY CASE GETS NEW LIFE** — A three-judge panel of
   the 2nd US Circuit Court of Appeals revived President Donald Trump's
   bid to move his New York hush-money case to federal court, where he
   could seek dismissal on presidential-immunity grounds. The panel said
   the late Judge Hellerstein erred in blocking removal after conviction;
   state-court proceedings are paused meanwhile. Trump was convicted in
   2024 on 34 felony counts of falsifying business records.
   Source: AP, 23 Nov 2026 (updated 04:16 GMT+7, 24 Nov 2026).

4. **WOMEN'S WORLD CUPS AWARDED** — FIFA's Extraordinary Congress
   appointed the USA, Mexico, Costa Rica and Jamaica as hosts of the
   2031 FIFA Women's World Cup (first 48-team edition) and England,
   Northern Ireland, Scotland and Wales as hosts of the 2035 edition.
   Both bids were unopposed, approved by acclamation.
   Source: FIFA / AP / Reuters, 23 Nov 2026 (24 Nov NZT).

5. **COP31 ENDS WITH NO DEAL** — UN climate talks in Antalya, Turkey,
   collapsed on Sunday 22 Nov without an overarching agreement: the
   EU-led bloc rejected a final text that made no mention of fossil
   fuels; Russia and Saudi Arabia had blocked the language. The longest
   COP on record; talks deferred to Ethiopia next year. Fallout and
   analysis dominated 24 Nov coverage.
   Source: AP / NPR, 22–23 Nov 2026.

6. **CHESS CROWN ON THE LINE** — The FIDE World Championship begins
   today in Geneva: defending champion D. Gukesh (India, 20) faces
   challenger Javokhir Sindarov (Uzbekistan, 20) in a 14-game match,
   Game 1 at 15:00 CET, Sindarov with White. Youngest title match in
   history; $2.5m prize fund.
   Source: Chess.com / FIDE, 24 Nov 2026.

7. **DENMARK BACKS 'GHETTO LAW'** — Denmark's Supreme Court ruled the
   2018 "ghetto law" evictions were not ethnic discrimination, upholding
   the termination of 91 leases in Helsingor. The court found the
   "non-Western" criterion neutral, not a measure of ethnic origin.
   Source: Reuters, 23 Nov 2026.
